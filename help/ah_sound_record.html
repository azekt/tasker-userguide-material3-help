Record Audio

Record from the microphone to an SD card file.

File is the path to the file on the SD card. It will have extension .3gp added.

Source is where to record from. Not all sources are available on all hardware, for instance the Call source is not available on the G1.

The Max Filesize is the maximum size of the resulting audio file in kilobytes.

A zero value for Max Filesize places no limit, in which case the recording will not stop until the SD card is full or the Tasker action Record Audio Stop is performed.

Note: playback of the resulting files can be done with Tasker's Music Play action, amongst others.

Warning: in many countries, recording of individuals' speech without their consent is illegal.
