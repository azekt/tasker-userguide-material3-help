Sleeping

Google Play Services on your device "think" you're sleeping.

Related video

  • Tasker - Sleep Tracking

Tasker doesn't have any control over how this state is detected. Tasker simply receives events from Google Play Services and relays them back to Tasker and to your profiles.

If this condition doesn't work, please don't blame Tasker or the developer since I really can't control how it works. As such, please use it at your own risk.

Action Inputs

Minimum Confidence

This represents the minimum confidence your phone has that you're sleeping.

When your phone tries to guess if you are sleeping it has a certain confidence that its guess is true.

A higher confidence value means that it's more certain that you are sleeping.

Maximum Light

This represents the maximum amount of light around you to trigger this state on, based on the device's ambient light sensor readings.

Higher values indicate brighter surroundings, while lower values indicate darker surroundings.

Maximum Motion

This represents the maximum amount of device motion to trigger this State on, based on the device's accelerometer readings.

Higher values indicate more movement of the device.
